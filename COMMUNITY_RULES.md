# Community rules — Avoda

**Avoda** is a **community-run**, **unofficial** directory of people and
businesses under the MGC umbrella (MGC Main, MGC Global, MGCNLCA, and the
MGCNLCA Alumni Community).

The product name is **Avoda** (no MGC in the brand title). The audience remains
the MGC community. See [BRAND.md](./BRAND.md).

## Rules

1. **Must be limited to people actively affiliated with MGC / MGCNLCA.**  
   Listings are for businesses and professionals run by people with active
   affiliation to MGC Main, MGC Global, MGCNLCA, and/or the MGCNLCA Alumni
   Community. Do not list unaffiliated businesses.

2. **Anyone can access the site / directory.**  
   The marketplace is public. Visitors do not need affiliation to browse
   categories or business pages.

3. **We must never use official MGC or MGCNLCA logos or design elements.**  
   Branding, visuals, and copy must stay clearly unofficial. Do not reuse
   official seals, wordmarks, color systems, or other protected design assets
   from MGC or MGCNLCA.

## Features (product scope)

- List business / professional categories
- Category pages list businesses sorted alphabetically
- One page per business
- Business pages may include an outlink (website / external contact)
- Analytics (Plausible; set `PUBLIC_PLAUSIBLE_DOMAIN`)

## Out of scope

- Listing specific products or services as catalog items
