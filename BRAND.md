# Avoda — brand lock

**Product name (locked):** **Avoda**

## Meaning

Hebrew *avodah* (עבודה), spelled here **Avoda**: **work**, **worship**, and
**service** as one idea. The marketplace is where community members’ work is
shared and supported.

## Positioning (locked 2026-08-09)

| Surface | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **Brand name** | **Avoda** — no “MGC”, “New Life”, or school coding in the title |
| **Audience** | Still the **MGC community** (MGC Main, MGC Global, MGCNLCA, MGCNLCA Alumni Community) |
| **Official status** | **Unofficial** — community-run; not operated by MGC or MGCNLCA |
| **Visuals** | Never use official MGC / MGCNLCA logos or design elements |
