# Avoda — brand lock

**Product name (locked):** **Avoda**

## Meaning

Hebrew *avodah* (עבודה), spelled here **Avoda**: **work**, **worship**, and
**service** as one idea. The marketplace is where community members’ work is
shared and supported.

## Positioning (locked 2026-08-09)
